Start a WorkBuddy gateway and sync selected models to DSH.

- Choose WorkBuddy2API Hub or wb2api in the backend tab, where you can also install or update the gateway.
- For Hub, configure regions and credentials in the region and key tab.
- Select models in the models tab, turn on DSH model sync, then save and sync.

[Full documentation](https://github.com/zhiqiangme/dsh-plugins/tree/main/dsh-autostart-workbuddy#readme)
